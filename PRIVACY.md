# Privacy notice

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to data when you use Portfolio Health Check, the web app at
https://portfolio-health-check-ro.streamlit.app. It is a free, personal, educational project by
Adrian Neațu, who is responsible for the app's own code and for the feedback form.

## In short

- The app does not ask who you are and does not save your portfolio.
- The online version runs on Streamlit Community Cloud, which adds its own cookies and analytics.
- Feedback is anonymous and used only to improve the app.

## 1. The portfolio you enter

The positions you upload or type are kept only in the app's memory while you use it. The app
writes nothing to a file or a database, and the data is gone when you close the page. In the
online version, this memory is on the servers of the hosting service, Streamlit Community Cloud
(Snowflake), for the time you use the app. Run on your own computer, the data never leaves it.
"Download as CSV" saves a file only on your device, where you choose.

## 2. Online lookup

If the online lookup is on, the names and tickers you type, never the values, are sent from the
app's server to SEC EDGAR (the US regulator's public company database) and OpenFIGI, to fill in
sector, size, currency and region. You can turn the lookup off; the app then uses only its
built-in list.

## 3. Cookies and analytics

The app's own code sets no cookies and collects no usage statistics (Streamlit's usage
statistics are turned off in its settings). The page of Streamlit Community Cloud, which hosts
the online version, sets its own cookies: some it needs to run the app (such as a session
cookie) and some for its own analytics (such as Google Analytics, Segment and Heap). The author
does not receive this analytics data and cannot turn it off. It is covered by
[Streamlit's privacy policy](https://streamlit.io/privacy-policy). You can block these cookies
and trackers in your browser settings or with a tracker blocker; the app still works.

## 4. Feedback

"Give feedback" opens a Google form in a new tab. It asks for no name or e-mail and needs no
sign-in, so the author cannot tell who answered. Please don't write personal or portfolio
details in it. The answers are stored in the author's Google account, read only to improve the
app, never sold or shared, and deleted when no longer needed, at the latest 12 months after they
are sent. Google processes the form under its own privacy policy. The legal basis is the
author's legitimate interest in improving the app (GDPR, art. 6(1)(f)).

## 5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data about you, and
object to its use. Because the app saves nothing and the feedback is anonymous, there is usually
no data that can be linked to you. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection
authority, ANSPDCP (https://www.dataprotection.ro).

## 6. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions: open an
issue at https://github.com/ADRIAN-NEATU/portfolio-health-check/issues. Issues are public, so
please don't post personal details there; ask for a private way to reach the author instead.
