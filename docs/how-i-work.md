# How I work

I work alone, in a light version of Scrum.

- **Sprints** last one week, Monday to Sunday. Sprint 1 is the exception: Tuesday 27 Oct to Monday 2 Nov 2026.
- **Planning** (15 min, first day): I set the sprint goal and choose what goes into the sprint.
- **Daily** (5 min, end of each working day): what I did, what comes next, what blocks me, and my own feedback after using the app. Each piece of feedback becomes a new item in the backlog.
- **Review and retro** (20 min, last day): what I delivered, then Start / Stop / Continue.
- **Definition of Done:** the acceptance criteria are met, the automated tests pass, the app is checked on the sample portfolios, the documents are updated, and no real personal data is in the project.
- **Tools:** Jira is my board. The `docs/` folder is the project's memory.
